# Phase 10 — Live Operations

## Objective

Operate, learn from, and improve the released game safely over time.

## Lead and support

Lead: Producer. Support: Data Analyst, engineers, Game Designer, and Market Analyst.

## Required outcomes

- Health, retention, economy, incident, sentiment, and cost monitoring
- Versioned content and experiment plans with rollback criteria
- Sustainable update cadence and player communication

## Continuing gate

Each update must demonstrate product value, technical readiness, measurement, communication, and rollback plans. Live operations has no automatic terminal phase.
