# Phase 07 — Beta

## Objective

Validate the near-complete product with representative users, devices, services, telemetry, and operating procedures.

## Lead and support

Lead: Producer. Support: Data Analyst, Market Analyst, engineers, and affected design roles.

## Required outcomes

- Representative external or production-like testing
- Prioritized defect, retention, usability, balance, compatibility, and operations findings
- No uncontrolled scope growth

## Exit gate

Pass when beta evidence shows release risks are understood, critical issues have been resolved or explicitly accepted, and remaining work is bounded polish.
