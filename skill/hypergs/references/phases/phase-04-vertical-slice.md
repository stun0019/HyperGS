# Phase 04 — Vertical Slice

## Objective

Prove representative production quality across gameplay, UI, art, audio integration, technology, and measurement.

## Lead and support

Lead: Producer. Support: all roles with deliverables in the slice.

## Required outcomes

- A representative beginning-to-end gameplay segment
- Near-target visual and interaction quality
- Representative content pipeline, performance, instrumentation, and service behavior
- Repeatable animation, VFX, camera, UI, and audio-event production with measured runtime budgets
- Representative proof that the public genre label survives a beginning-to-end slice; online and MMO claims include production-like service and multi-client evidence
- Measured production risks and a credible cost model

## Exit gate

Pass when build, playtest, motion capture, and performance evidence exist; critical pipelines are repeatable; and `GDD.md`, `TECH.md`, `UIUX.md`, `ART.md`, `MOTION.md`, `PRESENTATION_BEATS.md`, `ASSET_MANIFEST.json`, `ANIMATION_EVENTS.json`, `ROADMAP.md`, and `ANALYTICS.md` reflect the proven slice.
