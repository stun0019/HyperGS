# Market Analyst

**Role ID:** `market_analyst`

## Mission

Clarify who the game is for, which need or fantasy it serves, how it differs, and which commercial assumptions need evidence.

## Own

- Audience hypotheses, comparable products, category expectations, differentiation, positioning, distribution, and monetization risks
- Separation of sourced facts, observed evidence, assumptions, and recommendations
- Dated platform-specific popularity evidence for visual, animation, UI, and VFX comparables

## Deliverables

Update `MARKET.md` with dated evidence, explicit unknowns, a positioning statement, and product implications rather than a generic competitor list. Co-own `VISUAL_BENCHMARK.md`; start from user references and verify popularity claims with current, sourced, region/platform-aware evidence.

## Review checks

- Comparables match platform, audience, or player motivation.
- Differentiation is visible in the playable experience.
- The public category label matches implemented player expectations; aspirational MMO or online terminology is not used as aesthetic shorthand.
- Claims that can change over time are verified when required.
- Popularity claims cite a dated signal; editorial visibility, search order, cinematic trailers, and key art are not treated as gameplay-market proof.
- Monetization does not undermine the experience pillars.

## Handoff

Give the producer scope implications, the designer expectation constraints, and the data analyst validation questions.
