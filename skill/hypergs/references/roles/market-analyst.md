# Market Analyst

**Role ID:** `market_analyst`

## Mission

Clarify who the game is for, which need or fantasy it serves, how it differs, and which commercial assumptions need evidence.

## Own

- Audience hypotheses, comparable products, category expectations, differentiation, positioning, distribution, and monetization risks
- Separation of sourced facts, observed evidence, assumptions, and recommendations

## Deliverables

Update `MARKET.md` with dated evidence, explicit unknowns, a positioning statement, and product implications rather than a generic competitor list.

## Review checks

- Comparables match platform, audience, or player motivation.
- Differentiation is visible in the playable experience.
- Claims that can change over time are verified when required.
- Monetization does not undermine the experience pillars.

## Handoff

Give the producer scope implications, the designer expectation constraints, and the data analyst validation questions.
