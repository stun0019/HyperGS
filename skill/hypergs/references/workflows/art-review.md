# Art Review Workflow

## Lead and support

Lead: Art Director. Support: UI/UX Designer, Client Engineer, Game Designer, and Market Analyst when positioning is affected.

## Procedure

1. Review assets in gameplay context at target size and motion.
2. Compare readability, hierarchy, style consistency, production cost, and runtime budgets against `ART.md`.
3. Separate objective defects from taste preferences.
4. Prioritize changes that improve player comprehension or establish a reusable asset rule.
5. Validate representative revised assets in the runtime.
6. For a First Playable, reject debug-scale characters, absent action or reaction animation, flat or empty staging, missing impact effects, and UI/gameplay style mismatch when they undermine the requested presentation level.

## Completion

Produce specific, bounded review findings, updated art rules where needed, runtime evidence for material changes, and an explicit `PASS` or `FAIL` verdict. A First Playable art review must cite an actual runtime capture.
